AI Virtual Companion

A personal interactive companion project exploring how a virtual character can respond visually to speech. The focus includes avatar presentation, voice-driven lip-sync, and the experience of interacting with an expressive digital character.

Project overview

Voice audio→Lip-sync processing→Animated avatar

Areas of development

Virtual avatar

Working with a 3D character as the visible interface for a digital companion.

Voice-driven lip-sync

Exploring synchronization between speech audio and avatar mouth movement.

Interactive experience

Investigating ways to make conversational interaction feel more natural.

Cross-device experiments

Testing approaches to avatar interaction across different environments.
